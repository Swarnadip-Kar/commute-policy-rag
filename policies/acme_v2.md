# Acme Corp Transport Policy
version: 2

## 1. Eligibility
Employees of grade L3 and above are eligible for office transport. Employees living within 30 km of the office are eligible regardless of grade.

## 2. Night shift and escort
Women employees dropped after 9 PM get a security escort in the cab. Drops after 11 PM need the transport desk to confirm the escort in advance.

## 3. Booking cut-off
Cab requests must be made at least 3 hours before pickup. Requests after the cut-off are rejected.

## 4. Ad-hoc trips and approvals
Ad-hoc trips outside the roster need approval from the reporting manager. Airport trips need approval from the department head.

## 5. Weekend and holiday pickups
Saturday pickups are allowed with reporting manager approval. Sunday and public holiday pickups are not provided.

## 6. No-show penalty
Three no-shows in a calendar month lead to a 7-day suspension of transport.
