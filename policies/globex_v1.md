# Globex Transport Policy
version: 1
## 1. Eligibility
Employees on shifts ending after 9 PM or starting before 6 AM are eligible.
Day-shift employees are eligible only if they live more than 15 km from the
office.
## 2. Night shift and escort
Women employees dropped after 10 PM get a security escort in the cab.
## 3. Booking cut-off
Cab requests must be made at least 2 hours before pickup.
## 4. Ad-hoc trips and approvals
Ad-hoc trips are approved by the Transport Admin. Airport trips need approval
from the Finance team.
## 5. Weekend and holiday pickups
No weekend cabs are provided, except for employees on the on-call roster.
## 6. Guest travel
A guest may travel in the cab if a seat is free and the host pays 300 rupees
per trip.
## 7. No-show penalty
Three no-shows in a month lead to a written warning. Five no-shows lead to a
14-day suspension.
