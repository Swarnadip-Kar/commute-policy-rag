# Acme Corp Transport Policy
version: 1
## 1. Eligibility
Employees of grade L3 and above are eligible for office transport. Employees
living within 30 km of the office are eligible regardless of grade.
## 2. Night shift and escort
Women employees dropped after 8 PM get a security escort in the cab. Drops
after 11 PM need the transport desk to confirm the escort in advance.
## 3. Booking cut-off
Cab requests must be made at least 4 hours before pickup. Requests after the
cut-off are rejected.
## 4. Ad-hoc trips and approvals
Ad-hoc trips outside the roster need approval from the reporting manager.
Airport trips need approval from the department head.
## 5. Weekend and holiday pickups
Saturday pickups are allowed with reporting manager approval. Sunday and
public holiday pickups are not provided.
## 6. No-show penalty
Two no-shows in a calendar month lead to a 7-day suspension of transport.
