# Initech Transport Policy
version: 1
## 1. Eligibility
All employees are eligible for transport. There is no grade or distance
requirement.
## 2. Night shift and escort
Women employees dropped after 9 PM get a security escort in the cab.
## 3. Booking cut-off
Cab requests must be made by 6 PM on the previous working day.
## 4. Ad-hoc trips and approvals
Ad-hoc trips need approval from the HR business partner.
## 5. Weekend and holiday pickups
Saturday and Sunday pickups are available to all employees without approval.
Public holiday pickups need HR approval.
## 6. No-show penalty
Each no-show costs the employee 200 rupees.
